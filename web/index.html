Hear the Graph

AI that lets a blind student hear a graph, and tells them when not to trust it.

Product

  • Overview
  • Open the app

Privacy

No accounts. Your files are deleted after 60 minutes, or when you choose "Delete my files now".

Credits

Sound and keyboard navigation by Chart2Music. Typeface Atkinson Hyperlegible Next by the Braille Institute. Chart reading by Gemini.

Keyboard shortcuts

Single-key shortcuts work only while the chart has focus, so they never clash with your screen reader. Press Tab to reach the chart, or use the "Explore points" button.

Chart shortcuts
Left RightPrevious or next point, value spoken
Home EndFirst or last point
] [Maximum or minimum of this series
Page Up Page Down or Up DownSwitch series
PPlay or pause the whole graph
SRead the summary
CRead the confidence and its reasons
VAsk a question by voice: speak after the rising tone
Shift + EndPlay from here to the end, point by point
Q EFaster or slower point-by-point play
SpaceRepeat the current point
HFull list of Chart2Music keys
EscapeLeave eyes-closed mode

Sound: higher pitch means a higher value. Sound moves from your left ear to your right as x increases. A soft hiss on a point means its value is uncertain.
